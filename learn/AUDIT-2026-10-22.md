## Headline

The layer is honest. Every one of the nine `Results` theorems is described by what its statement
says, with its hypotheses, in all four places a reader sees it (node body, node
"What this does and does not prove", Trust page, `site/index.html` + `README.md`). In particular:

* the three Euler theorems are named as the classical theorems and that is correct;
* `results_five_platonic_solids` is described as a Schläfli-**pair** classification with the
  diamond property assumed, no metric regularity, no construction of two of the five, no
  uniqueness — and it says so in the title, the summary, the caveats, the quiz and the table;
* `results_picks_theorem` is described as Pick for strictly convex lattice polygons **with the
  `Finset S` / finiteness hypothesis not discharged**, repeatedly and prominently;
* `results_pick_count_of_euler`, `results_fan_pick_of_euler` and `results_disk_pick_of_euler` are
  described as arithmetic, with the proof route given separately — `results_fan_pick_of_euler`'s
  node is even titled "The statement is (n − 2)/2 = n/2 − 1".

**No claim was found whose hypotheses smuggle the result while being presented as the classical
theorem.** Nothing was rated FAIL.

What I did find and fix: ~20 wrong *pointers* (a lemma cited under a name or file it does not have),
four over-strong or wrong incidental claims (finite-dimensionality, `edgeGcd` commutativity, the
`B = nVertices` trigger, a doc-file attribution), two ambiguous quiz questions, and one sentence on
the Trust page that contradicted a node. Details in **Fixes**.

## Remaining concerns / decisions for Michal

1. **`learn/data/decls.json` is the `import Results` closure, not the repository.** 1127 decls in 38
   modules; `Cube.lean`, `Octahedron.lean`, `MetricRegular.lean`, `PicksTriangulation.lean` and
   `Polyhedron.lean` are absent because `Results.lean` does not import them. Consequence: the nodes
   correctly cite `Cube.cube_face_counts`, `Octahedron.octahedron_incidence`, `Cube.regularTetra`
   etc., but a reader cannot click through to them, and "All declarations (1127)" is not all of them.
   I patched the About wording; the real fix is to add the missing imports to `ExtractDecls.lean` and
   regenerate — which needs `lake build`, so I did not do it.
2. **`lean/AXIOM_AUDIT.md` is stale and now contradicts the Trust page.** Its "Status as of
   2026-09-25" table lists `results_unit_square_planar_euler`,
   `results_unit_square_disk_triangulation_handshaking` and `results_shoelace_pick_form_of_unit_square`
   as Results declarations depending on `native_decide` axioms. Those declarations are no longer in
   `Results.lean` (which now has exactly nine theorems). The Trust page already explains this
   ("the earlier unit-square Pick declarations … are no longer exported"), but anyone who opens
   `AXIOM_AUDIT.md` reads the old verdict. One or two lines in `lean/AXIOM_AUDIT.md` would fix it —
   I did not edit `lean/`.
3. **`lean/ROADMAP.md` and `lean/UNIFIED_SPINE.md` lag the Lean.** ROADMAP line 69 still has
   `- [ ] Shoelace = Haar/Lebesgue` unchecked although
   `LatticeArea.volume_convexHullRegion_eq_ofReal_shoelace` is proved (the node says "discharged",
   which is true of the Lean). UNIFIED_SPINE's "Current gap" section says the general Pick form
   "still takes `hEuler_planar` as EP-spine hyp" — `results_picks_theorem` does not. The nodes quote
   these files accurately, so nothing on the site is wrong; the files are what want updating.
4. **Three cited lemmas are `private`.** `faceEulerSum_three_expand` (EulerPoincare.lean),
   `edgeLatticePoints_eq_endpoints` (LatticeFan.lean) and `schlafli_of_bounds` (Platonic.lean). I
   annotated the first as private; the other two are still sent to a reader who cannot see them from
   outside the module. Your call whether to de-privatise or to mark them in the nodes.
5. **The axioms table I could not re-derive.** Per the brief I only checked that the wording is not
   stronger than `#print axioms = [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`, and it is not: the Trust
   page states exactly that, dates it, names the toolchain and Mathlib pin (both match
