What's proved (Lean)

All nine paper-facing theorems of lean/Results.lean, described by what their statements say, hypotheses included. Each depends only on [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]. What each does and does not prove →

TheoremMeaning
results_euler_characteristicEuler–Poincaré: a full-dimensional convex polytope (convex hull of finitely many points) has Euler characteristic 1 plain English →
results_euler_relation_dimension_threeEuler's polyhedron formula V − E + F = 2 for a 3-dimensional convex polytope in a 3-dimensional real inner product space, proved from that characteristic plain English →
results_euler_polyhedronThe same in ℝ³, with exactly two hypotheses: p is a finite convex hull and has affine dimension 3 plain English →
results_five_platonic_solidsSchläfli-pair classification: a combinatorially regular convex 3-polytope (every face an s-gon, m edges at each vertex, and — assumed, not yet proved in general — every edge in exactly two faces) has (s, m) ∈ {(3,3), (4,3), (3,4), (5,3), (3,5)}; plus the (V, E, F) numbers of those five pairs. Not a construction of the five solids, and no metric regularity plain English →
results_picks_theoremPick's formula: area = |S| + B/2 − 1 for a strictly convex counter-clockwise lattice polygon with distinct listed vertices, for any finite set S equal to its interior lattice points (that such a finite S exists is not yet proved here) plain English →
results_picks_of_empty_primitive_fanArea = B/2 − 1 for a strictly convex lattice polygon with distinct listed vertices, primitive edges and no interior lattice points plain English →
results_pick_count_of_eulerBookkeeping arithmetic: from V = I + B, F = T + 1, V − E + F = 2, 2E = 3T + B, A = T/2 (all hypotheses), A = I + B/2 − 1 plain English →
results_fan_pick_of_eulerArithmetic identity (n − 2)/2 = n/2 − 1 for n ≥ 3 (its proof is routed through the disk Euler number of a fan) plain English →
results_disk_pick_of_eulerArithmetic: if A = (2I + B − 2)/2 (a hypothesis) and 3 ≤ n ≤ B, then A = I + B/2 − 1 (its proof is routed through disk-Euler-preserving moves) plain English →

Strategy

Lean (primary): follow Lawrence C. Paulson's AFP Euler's Polyhedron Formula (2023; HOL Light port, credited to Jim Lawrence) — hyperplane arrangements + cell complexes + Euler-characteristic invariance. Target: Euler–Poincaré for full-dimensional convex polytopes, then V − E + F = 2 in dimension 3.

Isabelle (comparison base): the same AFP entry is vendored under isabelle/afp-Euler_Polyhedron_Formula/. See the Plain English Lean ↔ AFP map. Do not invent a fresh Isabelle development.

Historical: isabelle/Poly100.thy is an earlier unfinished Isabelle attempt (Tverberg triangulation) predating the AFP entry — kept for provenance only.
