Strategy

Lean (primary): follow Lawrence C. Paulson's AFP Euler's Polyhedron Formula (2023; HOL Light port, credited to Jim Lawrence) — hyperplane arrangements + cell complexes + Euler-characteristic invariance. Target: Euler–Poincaré for full-dimensional convex polytopes, then V − E + F = 2 in dimension 3.

Isabelle (comparison base): the same AFP entry is vendored under isabelle/afp-Euler_Polyhedron_Formula/. See the Lean ↔ AFP map. Do not invent a fresh Isabelle development.

Historical: isabelle/Poly100.thy is an earlier unfinished Isabelle attempt (Tverberg triangulation) predating the AFP entry — kept for provenance only.
